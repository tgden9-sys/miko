# Ground magnetic fixtures — concept exploration

Five visual concepts for a small, fixed city fixture that MIKO can stand on. Each concept sheet shows neutral, red, and blue charge states. **Concept 04, the freight lock, is now selected for the playable prototype.** Its near-side-view game images are `../../Game/sprites/fixtures/freight_lock_neutral.png`, `freight_lock_red.png`, and `freight_lock_blue.png`.

## Interaction

1. MIKO charges the fixture red or blue.
2. He selects the opposite suit polarity and is drawn onto it.
3. He switches his suit to the matching polarity. The fixed fixture stays in place and MIKO is propelled upward.

The fixture should be fixed to the ground, broad enough for both feet, and low enough to read as ordinary city hardware. Its light strips communicate charge without advertising it as a launch pad.

## Designs

1. `01_utility_hatch.png` — shallow octagonal inspection cover; clear standing surface and edge indicators.
2. `02_tram_coupler.png` — short embedded rail assembly; best suited to transit and freight districts.
3. `03_drain_manifold.png` — circular street drain with a solid centre and visible induction coils.
4. `04_freight_lock.png` — cargo docking lock with two raised rear housings, a flush walk-on edge, and clear red/blue line lighting.
5. `05_cable_junction.png` — three-part sidewalk junction marker with a very low profile and a clear centre.

The selected **04** has a side-on silhouette for the 2D game view. Its rear housings draw behind MIKO while its collision target stays low, so he can walk onto the center. The separate neutral, red, and blue images make the raised lines and gentle center glow read clearly. The surrounding demo scene still needs a broader anime-first art pass; the fixture alone does not set the finished game's look.

## Image generation prompts

Built-in image generation, one distinct stylized game prop concept per prompt. The common brief was: a compact ground-based magnetic fixture for MIKO's 2.5D cyberpunk city, worn cream enamel and steel-blue industrial materials, a flat surface for a small robot to stand on, neutral/red/blue indicator states, large three-quarter view plus side silhouette, clean studio background, with no character, text, spring, rocket, or overt launch-pad styling.

- **01:** Municipal octagonal utility inspection hatch with a recessed magnetic core and illuminated inset rim.
- **02:** Low tram-track service coupler with two parallel rails around a flat tread plate.
- **03:** Street-drain manifold with segmented grate, solid central standing disc, and glimpsed copper windings.
- **04:** Freight-bay docking lock with two squat side jaws and a broad central footplate.
- **05:** Raised sidewalk cable-junction marker with three steel segments, concentric seams, and ground bolts.
