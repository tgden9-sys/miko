# Asset split for the 2.5D game

## 3D gameplay assets

- MIKO: existing animated gameplay mesh, same skeleton and animations.
- Magnetic cube: installed, optimized game copy with neutral/red/blue inset panels.
- Platforms, steps, moving platforms and lifts: geometry/collision with authored surface detail.
- Interactive switches, pressure plates, sliding industrial doors and climbable ladders: 3D where their movement, lighting or interaction benefits from it.
- Active enemies: planned as 3D animated characters/robots with simple collision bodies, smaller game meshes, appropriate LOD and opt-in camera activity management. No new enemy has been added to this opening lesson.

Most useful next imported models: pressure plate/switch, lift platform and a proper sliding-door mechanism. These belong to later puzzle teaching; the discarded green door is not restored in the opening room.

## Painted / 2D assets in 3D space

Layered sky/city/buildings and decorative substation machinery, non-interactive rear walls, painted detail/decals and signs, defocused foreground framing, UI. Rear safety rail cards are decorative and remain behind the player plane. Particles/effect cards live at different 3D depths rather than being one flat overlay.

## Wooden crate

The new clean metal frame can be reused for a metal-framed wooden-panel variant. For an ordinary wood crate, a separate model is preferable: real slats, joints and a distinct silhouette should communicate its different material/function. A wood colour painted onto the metal cube would not give that result. No wooden variant has been generated yet.
