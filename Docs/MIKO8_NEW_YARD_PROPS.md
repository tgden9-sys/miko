# Wooden crate and loader appearance study

Launch `Preview Side-on Mockup.cmd`. The wooden crate is at x5.8 and the loader at x11.8, both visible on the starting screen. The loader is scaled to 80% (1.48m overall height, with its base recessed 0.12m into the paving). The wooden crate's visible bounds and collision are 0.8m on every axis, matching the metal crate. Its pillars flank the walking lane in depth, and the deck collision meets ground level. MIKO can walk straight through in either direction. Both use their embedded PBR textures and simple static collision. The loader's high-jump activation has not been added.

The loader starts green. Face it within targeting range and hold/release Q (L1) for red or E (R1) for blue. This uses the existing charge-target controls and 15-second charge duration; expiry and plasma neutralisation restore green. A nonblocking Area3D handles targeting separately from the solid pillars, so charging does not obstruct walking. `miko8_loader.gdshader` masks the texture's green inserts, preserving the casing, normal map, metallic/roughness maps and wear. `miko8_loader_charge.gd` owns the charge state. GPU captures of all three states are `Builds/Validation/NewProps/loader_state_0.png`, `loader_state_1.png` and `loader_state_2.png`.

Original supplied GLBs are preserved under `Source/Props/Loader` and `Source/Props/WoodenCrate`. `Source/Props/prepare_yard_props.py` creates the game copies under `Game/models/props`: loader 45,000 triangles (from 399,909), crate 18,000 triangles (from 173,266). Scene orientation and placement live in `Game/scripts/miko8_yard_props.gd`.

`Game/scripts/review_miko8_yard_props.gd` loads the actual side-on scene, checks that both props have meshes and collision shapes, and captures the GPU-rendered 1920x1080 view at `Builds/Validation/NewProps/in_scene.png`. Final run passed with no stderr errors, including 280 physics frames of walking input in each direction, checking ground height and lane position throughout. It also checks crate collider size, actual hold/release targeting for both charge colours, neutralisation and charge expiry. Textures, scale, colour states and ground contact were visually inspected in the captures.
