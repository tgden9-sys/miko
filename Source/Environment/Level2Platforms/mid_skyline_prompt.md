# Mid-distance skyline generation

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide transparent-background mid-distance industrial skyline layer for the MIKO cel-shaded 2.5D platform game.
Create a continuous full-width industrial district silhouette, visible buildings across ALL of the image including the centre. Broad landscape about 3:1 aspect. Front elevation with only very slight side-face perspective; all architecture upright and grounded along a single horizontal bottom line. A dense rhythm of medium and tall utility buildings, taller rectangular service towers, a few water tanks, connected horizontal pipe bridges, chimney stacks, antennae and sparse restrained warm amber windows. Varied rooftop heights, highest towers around the middle and thirds, moderate smaller gaps between rooflines, but NO huge empty central space. Lower parts form a continuous opaque band of building walls all the way across. Upper background above roofs is true transparent alpha so the separate far bokeh city can show through.
Style: professional painted cel-shaded graphic novel background, carefully composed readable large architectural shapes, three broad lighting tones, fine restrained dark edges. Less detail and contrast than close foreground props. Distant muted cool slate-blue and desaturated blue-grey concrete and steel, a few dim warm ochre lights. Strong coherent industrial service-yard architecture, not a generic glass skyscraper city. Cool atmospheric depth but not fog washing away the silhouette. Avoid photorealism, noisy surfaces, saturated neon and overly sharp high-contrast front-wall detail. There should be no bokeh dots baked into this layer; those are already in the farther background.
Composition: skyline strip fills the full width edge-to-edge. Keep all the bottoms at the bottom image boundary, no visible floor, no foreground objects, no framing border, no floating buildings. Transparent empty space only above skyline, never a central empty hole. No text, signs, logos, characters, watermarks or annotations. Genuine transparent background, do not paint a checkerboard.
