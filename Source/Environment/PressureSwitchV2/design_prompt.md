Create one polished landscape concept sheet, on a quiet deep slate-grey background, showing the SAME single low-profile floor pressure plate in two states side by side. Left: inactive. Right: pressed by a steel cargo crate, with clear vivid purple activation.
Design: a broad approximately 2-metre-wide steel pressure plate integrated into a small rectangular patch of worn concrete paving. Its perimeter is a continuous very shallow bevel/ramp, smoothly meeting the paving. The large central solid rectangular tread panel fills most of the unit and has only 2 centimetres of travel. SEALED overlapping metal edge skirts cover the moving seam: no exposed trench, empty recess, holes, raised rim, upright posts, or gaps that can catch a crate. The central tread panel sits almost flush; even when pressed it stays above a continuous support surface. The edge at the approach is extremely low and smoothly tapered, so a character walks straight onto it and a flat-bottomed metal crate slides directly across it.
Visibility: a bold large central mechanical diamond / pressure emblem, pale silver graphic in the inactive state, thick bright purple perimeter strips and a purple central symbol in the active state. On the front bevel facing the game camera, include a broad visible strip of indicator windows: dim grey when off, bright purple when pressed. Purple is the dedicated activation signal; do not add red/blue/green lights. Active effect is restrained low purple glow and a few small purple motes close to the plate, not giant energy columns.
Show both units at the same three-quarter view from a modestly elevated front angle, large enough to inspect the sealed edges. On the right, a single simple squat steel crate sits halfway onto the plate to demonstrate sliding onto it; keep enough of the plate and purple front indicator visible. Left plate unobstructed to show tread design. Near the bottom add a small clean side-profile inset showing the very shallow tapered approach and overlapping closed seam in the two heights; it must show a supported continuous load-bearing top, not a pit.
Style: professional restrained cel-shaded graphic-novel game asset concept, three broad lighting tones, confident dark edges, detailed but readable steel panels, recessed fasteners, subtle wear, muted cool steel-grey, warm grey paving, no brown rust-dominant finish. Coherent with an industrial service-yard game rather than futuristic glass sci-fi. Flat horizontal ground.
Text: only two small crisp labels, "READY" over left and "PRESSED" over right. No other text, dimensions, logos, watermark, or user interface. Prioritise coherent manufacturable geometry and a highly readable purple active state.
