## Change

The old raised plinth, front socket crossbar and front anchor shoes could obstruct MIKO's ladder approach. The revised concept lowers the footing, adds a broad shallow integrated approach ramp, opens the centre front of the receiving collar, and moves tall anchor shoes to the rear. Front fixings are flush and outside the footpath.

This is an image reference, not a tested 3D assembly. In Blender, match the mast seating planes and provide clear space between the ladder and the receiving collar. During placement, align the approach edge with the walking surface. Ensure the ramp/edge collision permits approach from the horizontal gameplay lane as well as the depicted front; bury or bevel any remaining low exposed side lip rather than creating a step. Set ladder bottom and entry markers from the final supporting surface, then verify the existing entry/exit animations and foot clearance.
