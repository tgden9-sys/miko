# Modular tower — three Trellis reference images

Created 4 November 2026 using the built-in image generation tool. Each final is a separate single-object PNG with genuine alpha transparency.

Final deliverables:

1. `01_base_four_fixings_v3.png`: revised low footing with shallow approach ramp, open-front collar and four mast brackets (two tall rear and two low front). This supersedes the v1 and v2 bases; see `base_four_fixings_v3_prompt.md` for its prompt and notes.
2. `02_middle_v1.png`: repeatable mast bay, with open ends and the front kept clear for a separate ladder.
3. `03_top_v2.png`: flat cap landing deck, with gussets and short connector below.

Generate each as a separate Trellis asset. They form a shared design family, not dimensionally guaranteed meshes. After reconstruction, the mating planes, section dimensions, connecting sleeves and origins need matching in Blender before stacking. Proposed scale targets remain approximately 0.8 m square core, 1.0592 m repeat height (four 0.2648 m ladder intervals), 1.4 m square base, and 2.0 by 1.6 m top deck. Final scale and landing depth must be checked against MIKO's existing ladder exit. Ladder and side-deck attachments remain separate future assets.

The v1 top file is an earlier cutout iteration; use v2 for the delivered set. A soft background colour may appear in viewers that ignore alpha; the inspected pixels outside the physical object have alpha zero and are transparent in the PNG.
