# Wake Lab room layout

The panorama establishes the visual design. Build the actual room **at least 60 metres wide**: three 20-metre camera widths at the tutorial's current 11.25-metre orthographic height and 16:9 aspect ratio. The camera scrolls at the existing scale; the panorama is not a single gameplay view.

| Room span | Route |
|---|---|
| 0–20 m | Left wall passage, wake dock, and a clear run-up. |
| 20–40 m | Staggered maintenance platforms climb to a short raised catwalk, then return to the main floor. |
| 40–60 m | Ground-level run-out to the right wall passage. |

For room connections, use matching floor heights and side-wall passage dimensions. Place the transition beyond the threshold at each room boundary, with the destination spawn just inside its matching passage. The wall and window frames stay fixed to the room. Containment machinery behind the windows shares one slower parallax layer.
